Working While Black

Race, identity and inequality in the American workplace.

Data visualizations examining the pay gap, career advancement and economic inequality experienced by Black women.

Black Women Earn Less Than White Women

In 2025, Black women working full time earned a median of $942 per week, compared with $1,108 for White women.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, 2025 annual averages.

Black Women Face a Promotion Gap

For every 100 men promoted to manager, only 60 Black women were promoted. This highlights differences in opportunities for career advancement.

Source: McKinsey & Company and LeanIn.org, Women in the Workplace 2025.

The Wage Gap for Black Women Across the United States

Black women working full time, year-round earn less than White, non-Hispanic men. The gap varies across states.

Source: National Women's Law Center.
